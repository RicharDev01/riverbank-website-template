Stewardship Partnership

Ongoing guidance for clients who value continuity, accountability, and informed decision-making.

The Stewardship Partnership is a high-touch, ongoing advisory relationship designed for clients who want consistent support as their financial lives evolve.

Schedule a Stewardship Conversation

The Stewardship Partnership is a high-touch, ongoing advisory relationship designed for clients who want consistent support as their financial lives evolve.

What the Partnership Provides

  • Regular strategic check-ins to assess progress and adjust direction
  • Ongoing financial planning support across life stages
  • Behavioral coaching to support better financial decisions
  • Coordination support with external professionals (as appropriate)
  • Access to planning tools and scenario modeling

This relationship is designed to support long-term resilience, not short-term optimization.

All Riverbank services are designed using the RIVER Method™ and applied through the Financial Resilience Framework. Engagements are distinct. Participation in one does not imply transition to another.

Engagement Structure

Flat-fee or subscription-based pricing
Quarterly or scheduled review cadence
Clear scope and expectations
Transparent boundaries around services

Who It’s For

Individuals & Families

Seeking long-term clarity and steady accountability.

Values-Driven Clients

Preferring structured guidance without commission-based incentives.

Navigating Change

Moving through transition, complexity, or growth with strategic direction.
